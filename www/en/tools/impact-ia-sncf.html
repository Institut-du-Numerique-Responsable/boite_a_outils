Impact'IA (SNCF)

Environmental footprint calculator for large language models, with a detailed methodology document. Covers thirty generative AI models from Google, Anthropic, OpenAI and Mistral, reporting greenhouse gas emissions, water and electricity use per token, with a life-cycle view and reduction practices. Built jointly by SNCF, Resilio and Wavestone.
