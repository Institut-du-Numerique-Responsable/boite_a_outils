RGESN — French general framework for digital service eco-design

France's reference framework for eco-designing digital services, 2024 version led by Arcep with ADEME, Arcom, DINUM, CNIL and Inria. Organised into verifiable criteria spanning strategy, specification, architecture, content, front-end, back-end and hosting. Underpins eco-design declarations.
