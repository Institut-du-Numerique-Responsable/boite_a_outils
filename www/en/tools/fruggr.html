Fruggr

Fruggr is an automated assessment platform for the environmental and social footprints of digital services. Thanks to its many KPIs, its objective is to improve the impact of companies' information systems by implementing a continuous improvement process. The license is commercial for the moment, even if in the future part of the code (the calculation engine) will be released in Open Source for the sake of transparency and sharing with the community.
