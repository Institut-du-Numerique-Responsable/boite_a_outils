EcoLogits

Open source Python library estimating the energy consumption and environmental footprint of calls to generative AI models (OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, Google, Hugging Face). Drops into existing code and surfaces the impact of every request, whether text, image or video. Maintained by GenAI Impact.
