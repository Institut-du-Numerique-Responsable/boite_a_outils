Accessible office documents (rgaa)

Create accessible office documents: The RGAA applies to any document or application available online: Internet, Intranet and web applications, but also content available for download. In the RGAA consultation theme, criterion 13.7 (level A) stipulates that "each office document for download has, if necessary, an accessible version". Compliance with this criterion can be achieved in particular by providing an accessible HTML version or by making the document accessible in the format offered for download.
