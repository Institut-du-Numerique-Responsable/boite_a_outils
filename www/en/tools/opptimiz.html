oPPTimiz

PowerPoint add-in developed by EDF that shrinks presentations by compressing images and removing unused slide masters. Adds a Sustainable IT group to the ribbon, with a shortcut to Microsoft's accessibility checker. Also works from the command line and Windows Explorer. GPL-3.0.
